How it works

1. Open your invoice list

In Xero, go to Sales → Invoices. Use Xero's own tabs and date filters to narrow down what you need.

2. Tick, or select all pages

Checkboxes appear next to each invoice. One link selects every invoice across all pages of the list.

3. Get one tidy zip

Each invoice is saved as its own PDF, named with its number and contact, with attachments alongside, in month folders.

Built for bookkeepers and accountants

Year-end handovers, audits, client requests, moving to new software: anywhere you need individual invoice PDFs, InvoiceZip saves hours of clicking.

Private by design

InvoiceZip runs inside your own browser and uses your existing Xero login. It doesn't connect to Xero's API, doesn't need admin rights, and never sends your invoices or financial data to us or anyone else. Files go straight from Xero to your computer.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

InvoiceZip is a browser extension. This policy explains what data it handles and what it doesn't.

What the extension does with your data

  • It reads the invoice list and invoice files from your Xero account, using the login you already have open in your browser, only when you ask it to download.
  • It saves those files directly to your computer as a zip file.
  • Your invoices, contacts, amounts and attachments are never sent to us or to any third party. We have no server that receives them.
  • Your Xero login details are used only inside your browser, for requests to Xero itself, and are never stored permanently or shared.

Licensing and payments

  • If you buy a license, Dodo Payments processes your payment and handles your billing details (name, email, payment information) under its own privacy policy. We don't see or store your card details.
  • To check that a license is valid, the extension sends your license key to Dodo Payments' license service. No invoice data is included.
  • We receive your email address from Dodo Payments to provide support and send license-related messages.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking of your Xero data.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No advertising.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your email and license records at any time by contacting us at the address below. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its local data from your browser.
